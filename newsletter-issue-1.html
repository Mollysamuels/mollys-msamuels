A SHORT STORY

Simi and the 2:30 interview

Simi had it all planned. Interview on Thursday at 2:30, printed copies of her CV, and her favourite navy outfit pressed and ready.

Then, on Monday evening, she opened her wardrobe and remembered. Everything good she owned was at the laundry, and the laundry wasn't giving it back until Friday. One day too late.

She stood there in her slippers, going through what was left. Nothing she'd wear to face a panel. Nothing that said hire me.

Then she remembered the gown she'd bought from Molly Samuels a few months back, the one piece in her wardrobe that made her stand a little taller. She grabbed her phone, opened the site, and went straight to Gowns.

Twenty minutes later she'd chosen a gown, a pair of heels, and a bag that actually matched. It was an Abuja order, which meant it would be at her door within 24 hours. She pressed place order and finally breathed out.

On Thursday, whatever the panel asked, outfit worries wouldn't be one of the things on her mind.
