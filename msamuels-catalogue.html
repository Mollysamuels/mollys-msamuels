Design your uniform

Four steps

  1. Choose your items

    Pick from blazers to socks and note each product code, for example MS-BLZ-01.

  2. Choose your colours

    Use the colour key and quote the code, for example NVY. Ask for a swatch.

  3. Add your logo

    Send your crest and design. Polos, sportswear, socks and ties can carry your own logo and design.

  4. Request your quote

    Use the bulk order form or call us. We reply with a quote.

Terms

Prices
All prices are in Naira and include 7.5% VAT. Delivery and shipping are not included.
Minimum order
100 to 200 pcs for most items. Socks and ties: 200 pcs.
Sizes
All uniforms are produced to standard size charts. See the size guide.
Made in
Large-volume blazers and shirts, all ties and all tartan are produced in China. Production 35 to 60 days, shipment 8 to 10 weeks. Other items are produced in Nigeria.
Collaboration price
Applies when schools produce together, over 1,000, 2,000 or 3,000 pcs.
