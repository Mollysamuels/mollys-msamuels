Returns & Exchanges

Last Updated: 1 October 2026

We want customers to have a satisfactory shopping experience with Molly Samuels. This policy explains the general conditions applicable to returns, exchanges, and refunds for products purchased through our website. This policy is subject to applicable consumer protection laws. Where the law provides a customer with rights more favourable than those stated here, the applicable legal rights will apply.

1. Eligibility for Returns

A product is generally eligible for return within 14 days of delivery where:

  • The wrong product was delivered;
  • The product is defective or damaged upon arrival;
  • The product does not reasonably correspond with its description; or
  • The return otherwise qualifies under the applicable terms of sale.

Returned products should generally be unused, unworn, unwashed, and in their original condition, with original packaging, labels, and tags where applicable.

2. Clothing and Fashion Items (Mollys)

For clothing and fashion products, customers should take reasonable care when trying on items.

Products that have been worn, washed, altered, damaged, stained, or otherwise used may not qualify for return unless the product is defective or applicable law provides otherwise.

3. Uniforms (M. Samuels)

Uniform products may have specific requirements relating to size, fitting, school requirements, or other specifications.

Customers should carefully confirm applicable size and product information, using our Size Guide, before completing an order.

Personalised, altered, embroidered, bespoke, or specially prepared items may not be eligible for return unless they are defective or otherwise covered by applicable consumer protection laws.

4. Requesting a Return

Customers should contact us within 14 days of receiving an order if they wish to request a return.

The customer may be required to provide:

  • Order number;
  • Name;
  • Description of the issue;
  • Photographs or other evidence where relevant; and
  • Other information reasonably required to assess the request.

5. Refunds

Where a refund is approved, the refund will generally be processed using the original payment method via Paystack where reasonably possible.

The time required for the refund to appear in the customer's account may depend on the payment provider or financial institution.

Delivery charges may or may not be refundable depending on the reason for the return and applicable law.

6. Exchanges

Where an exchange is available, the replacement product may be subject to availability.

If the requested replacement is unavailable, the customer may be offered another appropriate resolution.

7. Incorrect or Damaged Products

If we send an incorrect product or a product arrives damaged or defective, customers should contact us as soon as reasonably possible.

We may request photographs or other information to help us investigate the issue.

Where the issue is confirmed, we may offer an appropriate remedy, which may include replacement, exchange, repair, refund, or another remedy required by applicable law.

8. Non-Returnable Items

Where permitted by applicable law, certain products may not be eligible for return, including:

  • Personalised or bespoke products;
  • Altered products;
  • Products made specifically to a customer's specifications;
  • Products that have been worn or used;
  • Products damaged after delivery through customer misuse; or
  • Products that cannot reasonably be resold for hygiene or other legitimate reasons.

9. Return Shipping

Where the return is due to an error on our part or a confirmed defective product, we will cover reasonable return delivery costs where applicable.

Where a return is requested for reasons unrelated to a fault or error, the customer may be responsible for return delivery costs.

10. Refund Processing Time

Once an approved return has been received and assessed, we will initiate the applicable refund or replacement process within a reasonable period.

The time required for the funds to reach the customer may depend on the payment provider or bank.

11. Bulk and Custom Orders

Bulk uniform orders and custom orders placed via our Custom Order or Bulk Order request forms may be subject to separate terms agreed directly with you at the time of order, due to their made-to-specification nature.

12. Policy Changes

We reserve the right to update this Return, Refund & Exchange Policy from time to time. Any updated version will be published on this website.
